### ✧ a little about me

I’m Loveyy. I’m drawn to creative web and desktop development: shaping interfaces, making digital spaces feel personal, and learning how apps come together. My style sits somewhere between thoughtful and a little whimsical—polished enough to use, but never too serious to have a tiny heart in the corner.

Outside the code: music, films, games, stories, guitars, late-night ideas, and worlds I can happily disappear into.
